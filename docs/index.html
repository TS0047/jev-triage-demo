Read this before believing anything above

This is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. It is an architecture demo for calibrated decision models. Patients are synthetic and answer from a scripted profile; the runs measure question-selection behaviour, not diagnostic accuracy.

Finding likelihoods are hand-authored plausible estimates, not epidemiological data. The differential covers six tropical febrile illnesses plus an escape hatch — any real presentation outside it must escalate, which is what the lupus case demonstrates. Findings are treated as conditionally independent, which is false in reality: adequate for ranking, wrong as a reported posterior.

In every case tested, including those at 100% confidence, the model rated safe_to_act_without_clinician at 0.03–0.04. The system is designed to hand off to a human, never to conclude.
