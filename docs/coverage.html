What falls outside — and what happens then

Any fever that is not one of the six above is outside this demo's world. The model is asked every round whether the presentation lies outside the differential; at 0.60 or above the consultation stops and escalates instead of forcing an answer.

This was tested directly. A lupus presentation — malar rash, photosensitivity, oral ulcers, symmetric arthritis, proteinuria with red cell casts, every tropical serology negative — was given to a closed version with no escape hatch. It answered "dengue" at 0.42 after three wasted rounds. With other available, the same input returned "other" at 1.00 and escalated on round one.

That is why widening the list is the second fix, not the first. Any bank is finite; patient N+1 always walks in with something you did not list. The escape hatch is what makes the system safe at any bank size.

On these numbers

The likelihoods here are hand-authored plausible estimates, not epidemiological data. They are adequate for ranking which question to ask next, which is all the loop uses them for. They are not adequate as a reported posterior, and the Bayes update additionally assumes findings are conditionally independent — which is false in reality, since rash and petechiae, or jaundice and dark urine, travel together. Real deployment needs sourced priors and a correlation-aware update.

In every case tested, including those the model scored at 100% confidence, it rated safe_to_act_without_clinician at 0.03–0.04. The system is built to hand off, never to conclude.
